        Architecture

    flowchart TB
  subgraph FE[Frontend]
    CHAT[AI chat] --- PREV[Preview A/B] --- MIX[Mixer] --- USE[Use voice]
  end
  CHAT --> GEN[Voice Profile Generator: Claude or offline interpreter]
  ANA[Voice analysis] --> GEN
  REF[Reference target] --> GEN
  GEN -->|validated changes| STORE[(Voice Profile store: single source of truth)]
  MIX -->|edits| STORE
  STORE --> ENG[Browser engine: dsp.js]
  STORE -->|REST + WebSocket| SRV[voice_engine server]
  SRV --> CPU[CPU backend: numpy/numba, sample-matched to dsp.js]
  SRV --> ROCM[ROCm backend: torch on AMD GPU]
  ENG --> OUT[Headphones]
  SRV --> OUT
  ROCM -.next.-> NEXT[Learned denoiser, Silero VAD, whisper.cpp, virtual microphone]

    
        
    Not available in this browser build: virtual microphone output (a web page cannot create an audio device), formant measurement, and learned models such as Silero VAD or a neural denoiser. Their slots in the chain are in place for the ROCm backend.
